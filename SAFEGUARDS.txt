Safeguards for the GMPP quarterly pipeline

- A dry run (--dry-run) only detects files. It does not write into /Users/mac/GMPP
  or /Users/mac/Desktop/gmpp-data, it does not train, and it does not upload.
- The existing files in /Users/mac/Desktop/gmpp-data are read only.
- If a new spreadsheet has an unknown column layout, the job stops, writes
  NEW LAYOUT: human review needed, and notifies you. It will not clean, train,
  or upload until you run:  python3 gmpp_pipeline.py --approve-layout
- Training output goes to GMPP-pipeline/candidates/, not over your current files.
- The live Hugging Face model is never changed during setup. A scheduled run may
  upload only after a known or approved layout and a successful training run,
  and only if a Hugging Face token is already available on the Mac. The previous
  Space model is backed up under model-backups/ first.
- Do not put a token in this folder, in config.json, or in the LaunchAgent plist.
